# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone who wants to
spend less time doomscrolling and more time living. We pledge to make
participation a respectful experience regardless of age, body, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind and patient with newcomers.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Keeping comparisons to other apps (Brick, Blok, Bloom, Foqos, etc.) **honest
  and fair** — no trashing competitors.
- Focusing on what's best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately misleading claims.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, and PRs that violate this
Code of Conduct, and may ban repeat offenders. To report an issue, open a
private report via the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** flow
or contact the maintainer listed on
[getdetach.app](https://getdetach.app/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=shell_repo).
