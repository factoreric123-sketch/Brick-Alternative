## How they block

All of these use a **tap-to-block** NFC mechanic and, on iOS, Apple's **Screen
Time / Family Controls** layer to enforce blocks. The differences are price,
platform, and depth of features — not magic. None can make blocking *literally*
unbypassable on a phone you fully control; they remove the *easy* bypasses.

## The honest rundown

### Detach — cheapest, iOS-first, no account
Free app, iOS 17+, no account or email. System-level blocking with **no in-app
bypass button**. Optional **$9.99** NFC card, or bring your own tag. Best if you
mainly want to stop doomscrolling on iPhone for as little as possible.
**Trade-off:** iOS only (no Android), and it's a newer, leaner product.

### Brick — premium, cross-platform, feature-rich
Free app + a **~$71** battery-free NFC device. Works on **iOS and Android**.
Deep **Modes** system (Work/Study/Sleep), **Strict Mode** (blocks app deletion
mid-session), scheduled auto-brick, and Safari site-blocking on iOS. Polished
hardware and an established brand. **Trade-off:** the priciest option here.

### Blok — mid-priced NFC blocker
A **~$59** NFC blocker in the same tap-to-lock family. **Trade-off:** costs far
more than Detach's optional card for a similar core mechanic; check its current
platform support and feature set on its own site.

### Bloom — screen-time focused
Around **~$47**, positioned around conquering screen time. **Trade-off:** again,
pricier than a free app + $9.99 card; verify its current features directly.

## Which should you pick?

- **Want it free / cheapest on iPhone?** → **Detach** (free app, optional $9.99 card).
- **Need Android or a deep Modes/Strict system?** → **Brick** (~$71, cross-platform).

- **Comparing Blok/Bloom?** → fine products, but at $59/$47 you're paying more
  than Detach's free-app-plus-$9.99-card route for the same tap-to-block habit.

We're not knocking any of these — Brick in particular is genuinely good. This
repo just exists to make the **cheap, honest, iOS-first** option easy to find.
